      Live screen
      
        
      Off by default. When on, the screen shows the live layer of your On screen theme (clock, music, PC stats, text and pictures) drawn by CoolerScreen. What is shown is set per theme, in On screen. Closing the window then keeps CoolerScreen running in the tray (the icon near the clock); use Quit in its menu to end it, and the screen goes back to its stored loop. Sending a theme or restarting the screen pauses live and brings it back afterwards.
      
        
      
          
      
          
      
        
      
        
      
          
      
            
      
          
      
            
      Recommended. The window takes a moment longer to come back from the tray.

      Advanced
      
          
      Restoring the original driver lets the vendor app (LOVINGCOOL MONITOR) see the screen again. The screen then switches its display off 30 seconds after any program closes its connection.
